A mobile app built for Takoyae Ramen, a street-food business in Sukmajaya, Depok — bringing a small food stall's menu and ordering experience onto customers' phones.

Building for a real small business is a different discipline from enterprise work: the users are customers, not employees; the app has to sell the food, not just record data. Every screen has to earn its place, and it has to work on the low-end Android phones people actually carry.
