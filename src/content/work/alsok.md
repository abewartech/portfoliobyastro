As a Full Stack Developer at ALSOK Indonesia (formerly Shield On Service) — a Japanese integrated facility management and security outsourcing company — I own several internal applications used across the whole organization:

- **Attendance system** — serving roughly 30,000 employees across many branches. At that scale, "just a CRUD app" stops being true: performance, data consistency, and edge cases in shift/branch logic are the real work.
- **Carpooling app** — coordinating employee transport.
- **E-learning platform** — training delivery and tracking.
- **IFMS (Integrated Facility Management System)** — the operational backbone for facility management services.

Enterprise internal tools live or die on reliability and clarity — thousands of non-technical users depend on them daily, and there's no "refresh and try again" culture to hide behind.
