Two related builds in the social-listening space:

- **TrenSaham** — monitors stock-related conversation on social media, surfacing what's being talked about and how sentiment moves.
- **Scraper** — a general-purpose web app for scraping social media, with storage and presentation of the collected data.

The interesting engineering here isn't the scraping itself — it's doing it responsibly and reliably: rate limits, changing page structures, deduplication, and turning noisy raw captures into something a human can actually read and act on.
