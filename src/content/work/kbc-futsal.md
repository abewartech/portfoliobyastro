My undergraduate thesis at Universitas Indraprasta PGRI: an online booking system for futsal field rentals at KBC Futsal, built as a full stack:

- **Android app** — the customer-facing booking experience.
- **Web backend** — field management, schedules, and transactions.
- **Lumen API variant** — a rebuilt backend on Laravel's Lumen micro-framework, exploring how the same domain behaves as a lean API.

It covers the whole arc — from problem statement and system design through implementation and thesis defense — and it's where a lot of my full-stack instincts were formed.
